Corpus-level benchmarks. Scores the FINISHED graphs of both tracks against ground truth;
runs after the pipeline, never as part of it.

Files:
  10_mine_benchmark.ipynb   corpus - MINE-1 recall: are the 15 facts per article findable in the graph?

Ground truth lives in dataset/metadata/<art>.json ("facts": 15 sentences per article).
Per-fact judgment logs land next to each graph scored (mine_judgments_<variant>.json / .csv);
corpus reports in results/<model>/_global/evaluation/mine_report_<judge>_<run_tag>.json. One pass
scores every (model, variant, article) named in the notebook's MODELS/GRAPH_VARIANTS and resumes
past anything already scored, so a sweep and a single-model run are the same cell.

Pairs with nb9 (grounding): nb9 measures precision (is the graph true to the article?),
nb10 measures recall (is the article in the graph?). Quote them together.
